# Mightier than the Sword — current rules

Generated from `src/data/content.json`. Source edition pnp-v15. See DECISIONS.md for digital rulings.

## Setup

Use all 9 books in a random 3-by-3 grid. Each book has 2 pages. Edge-adjacent pages are 1 step apart, including the two halves of a book. Each player takes a protagonist, 10 Inklings and 12 matching-color memories (3 per row). Start with 4 Inklings in supply; reserve 3 for Act II and 3 for Act III. Cover all row upgrades with memories. Start at 0 points, with no characters. Shuffle the shared decks; deal 2 Twists and 1 face-up Subplot each. Place protagonists on any pages. The base pool has 15 conflict tokens, 5 per Act. Randomly select 1 per player for EACH Act; set the others aside. Put Act I's selected tokens on the main board. Choose a first player; turns run clockwise.

## Your turn

If your supply is empty at the start, shoot the moon (below). Otherwise move your protagonist up to your movement limit; staying is allowed. Then choose ONE action: place Inklings on its page using your Resolve level; upgrade one of your Inklings there; OR put 1 supply Inkling in an empty activation square on a character card and use that action. Characters move only as their abilities allow and carry no Inklings. You may play 1 ON YOUR TURN Twist after moving, before or after your action; discard it. Finish rewards and Subplots, resolve full books, then discard to your Twist hand limit.

## Inklings and characters

Place each Inkling in an empty numbered space on your active page; if that page is full, use the book's shared overflow. Bonus placements also earn memory rewards; moving Inklings does not. Erase returns Inklings to their owner's current supply. Suspend sends an Inkling to that owner's NEXT Act reserve (out of play in Act III). Foreshadow takes Inklings from your next Act reserve into supply; if unavailable, gain 1 point per Inkling instead. Gain a character by drawing the top card and placing its figure with your protagonist. Characters and protagonists occupy no slots. Card Inklings stay until erased or the Act ends. A character move follows adjacent pages unless it says any page. Outside conflict, "here" means the acting character's page; overflow and conflicts belong to the whole book. If the character deck is empty, gain 2 points instead.

## Upgrade and share

Suspend one of your Inklings from a numbered space without a memory and replace it with the leftmost covered memory from any row. This improves that row level. The space stays empty until an Inkling is placed there. A memory adds no power and stays unless a card returns it. Return it to the rightmost uncovered space in its row, lowering that row level; any Inkling on it stays. Whenever an Inkling is PLACED on it, the Inkling's owner and memory's owner each gain its reward (once total if the same player). Resolve the placer's reward first. A Curiosity memory places the reward Inkling in overflow on that same book. Extra upgrades still suspend your Inklings; if impossible, gain 2 points instead.

## Subplots

Track only events that occur while your current Subplot is face up. Keep progress even if pieces move or disappear. When full, choose its character reward OR its alternative, then discard it and draw a new Subplot. The new card cannot count the event that completed the old one. Reshuffle Twist and Subplot discards when needed; draw only what is available.

## Conflict

A book fights when all numbered spaces across BOTH pages are filled with Inklings; Agamemnon can count for 1 empty space. All players with Inklings, characters or a protagonist there participate, even at 0 power. Lock in participants; finish even if Inklings are erased. Reveal any Trojan Horse power first, then book and character start effects. Each participant may play 1 CONFLICT Twist in clockwise order from the active player; a raised limit allows 2. Keep going until all pass or reach their limit. Add Inklings (1 each), character power, protagonist Valor and effects. Highest power wins; ties go to the earliest participant clockwise from the active player. Discard ALL played Twists. Resolve win/loss effects and book after-effects, erase all Inklings from that book, then give the winner rewards. Figures and memories stay. Resolve other full books in the active player's chosen order.

## Conflict rewards

EVERY conflict takes 1 available token from the current Act pool on the main board. The winner places it on the book and gains its strong bonus. If the book's +3 point space is uncovered, also score 3 times this new token's multiplier (Act I/II/III: x1/x2/x3), then cover that space. A covered book gives NO printed book points. Also gain each older token's face-up reward: its bonus if strong, or 1/2/3 points by its Act if on its back. Then flip all tokens there to their backs. Only the new token can multiply the book's points; never multiply token rewards. Choose the order of token rewards.

## Acts and victory

The last available token ends the Act: finish that conflict and its rewards, then end the turn. Leave other full books for the next turn; no conflict resolves without an available token. Or, if you START a turn with no supply Inklings, gain each unused current-Act token's strong bonus, without book points or multipliers; then end the Act. After Act III, most points wins; ties share victory. Otherwise flip tokens on books strong and uncover every book's +3 space, keeping its old tokens with it. Erase card Inklings, release the next reserve, prepare the Trojan Horse, and put the next Act's selected tokens on the main board. Leave book Inklings in place. The next player takes the next turn.

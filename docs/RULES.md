# Mightier than the Sword — current rules

Generated from `src/data/content.json`. Source edition digital-and-print-0.6, rules mightier-0.6.0. See DECISIONS.md for digital rulings.

## Setup

Shuffle 9 books; start with 3 in a triangle and stack the rest face down. Each player takes a Quill, 12 Inklings and 12 memories (3 per row). Start at 0 points, with 6 supply Inklings, 3 reserved for Act II and 3 for Act III. Shuffle the decks; deal 1 Twist and 1 hidden Subplot each. Place Quills on any pages. Randomly select 1 conflict token per player for each Act and display Act I's tokens front-side up. In a 2-player game, place a neutral Inkling in the first page space of the first book. It occupies the space and can trigger conflict, but never participates. Choose a first player.

## Your turn

If you start with no supply Ink, choose a book containing one of your Inklings and resolve a conflict there instead of taking a normal turn. Otherwise move your Quill at least 1 page, up to your movement limit, ending on a different page. Place an Inkling in an empty page space on the destination page; if none are empty, place it in that book's Binding. You may then replace one of your Inklings there with a memory. Character actions cannot be chosen directly: landing an Inkling on a memory is the only way to use one. At the end of your turn, draw Twists equal to 1 plus your Insight level. There is no hand limit.

## Inklings and placement

The spaces along each book are page spaces. Binding is the shared Binding area outside them: Binding Inklings add power but do not fill page spaces and receive no Twist check. Resolve upgrades may grant additional placement choices shown on the player board. Page spaces grant no automatic Twist reward. Erase returns an Inkling to supply; suspend sends it to the next Act reserve (or out of play in Act III). Foreshadow moves Inklings from the next reserve to supply; gain 1 point for each unavailable Inkling. Moving an existing Inkling grants no placement reward.

## Memories and upgrades

To leave a memory, suspend your Inkling from a page space without a memory and place the leftmost covered memory from one row there, improving that row. Memories add no power and do not fill spaces. When you later place an Inkling on any memory, choose either that memory's benefit or one action on a character you own. This is the only way to take a character action. Curiosity places its bonus Inkling in this book's Binding. Returning a memory lowers its row; an Inkling on it stays.

## Subplots

Keep your active Subplot hidden. Its objective checks the current board and may include Inkling positions. When fulfilled, reveal it and choose a character or its alternative reward, then keep the completed card for its printed 1–3 end-game plot points and draw a new hidden Subplot. At most one Subplot per player may complete in each player turn. Played Twists are also kept for their printed 1–3 end-game plot points; these points are not added to the scoreboard during play.

## Immediate conflict

Finish placement rewards, then immediately fight when the last page space gains an Inkling. All players with Inklings or figures there participate. Resolve start effects, then check occupied page spaces once from left to right. At each space, its current Inkling owner may play 1 Twist or pass. Skip empty, memory-only and neutral spaces; Binding grants no checks. Keep each played Twist for end-game scoring. Other full books wait until this conflict finishes.

## Scoring and rewards

After checks, total Inklings, character power, Quill Valor and effects. Most power wins. Break every tie in favor of the tied player whose Inkling is furthest left among the book's page spaces. Choose a current-Act conflict token. If it is front-side up, first and second place score 5/2 in Act I, 7/3 in Act II or 10/4 in Act III; then flip it. If it is already back-side up, first and second score 3/2, 4/2 or 5/2 respectively. Conflict tokens have no bonus effects or multipliers. A front-side conflict also publishes a new book touching at least 2 others; then place 1 supply Inkling in a touching old book's Binding. A book may conflict repeatedly in the same Act.

## Acts and victory

When every current-Act conflict token has been used on its front side, finish that conflict and end the Act. Clear and reset the conflict tokens; return character-card Inklings to supply; release the next reserve; prepare the Horse and display the next Act's tokens. Book Inklings and memories stay. After Act III, add the printed end-game points on every kept Twist and completed Subplot to each player's scoreboard points. Highest total wins; ties share victory.

## Book effects

Dracula marks the first page space with a castle. The Odyssey rewards a figure for leaving its book. Around the World moves an owned figure after placement. Jekyll may reduce points below zero. Twenty Thousand Leagues redirects an Inkling that would enter its Binding to an adjacent book's Binding instead.

# Mightier than the Sword — current rules

Generated from `src/data/content.json`. Source edition pnp-v15, rules mightier-0.5.0. See DECISIONS.md for digital rulings.

## Setup

Shuffle 9 books; start with 3 in a triangle: two side by side, one centered below. Stack the rest face down. Books use a hexagonal grid; each has 2 pages. A touching page edge or internal fold costs 1 move. Each player takes a protagonist, 12 Inklings and 12 memories (3 per row). Start at 0 points, with 6 supply Inklings, 3 reserved for Act II, 3 for Act III; cover every upgrade with a memory. Shuffle decks; deal 1 Twist and 1 hidden Subplot each. No starting characters. Place protagonists on any pages. From the base pool of 15 conflict tokens (5 per Act), randomly select 1 per player for EACH Act; set the rest aside. Display Act I's tokens. Choose a first player; turns go clockwise.

## Your turn

With no supply Inklings at the start, shoot the moon (below). Otherwise move your protagonist at least 1 page, up to your movement limit, ending on a different page from its starting page. Then choose ONE action: place Inklings on its page using Resolve; replace one of your Inklings there with a memory; OR place 1 supply Inkling on a character's empty activation square and use its action. Characters move only through abilities and carry nothing. Twists are played ONLY in conflict. Resolve immediate conflicts before continuing any remaining placements. Finish effects, then discard to your combined hand limit (Twists plus your active Subplot) and end your turn.

## Inklings and placement

Use empty numbered spaces on your active page; if full, use shared overflow. Numbered spaces have no automatic Twist reward. Gain any memory rewards and on-placement book effects, including on bonus placements. Moving an Inkling grants no placement rewards. Erase returns it to its owner's supply; suspend sends it to their NEXT Act reserve (out of play in Act III). Foreshadow moves Inklings from next reserve to supply; gain 1 point each if unavailable. Gain a character by drawing the top card onto your protagonist's page unless stated otherwise; empty deck gives 2 points. Figures fill no spaces, except Agamemnon's trigger exception. Characters move between adjacent pages unless stated otherwise.

## Memories and upgrades

Suspend your Inkling from a numbered space without a memory; replace it with the leftmost covered memory from any row, improving that row. The space becomes empty. Memories add no power, never fill spaces and grant no Twist check. An Inkling may later occupy a memory space; it counts normally. Its owner and the memory owner each gain that memory's benefit (once if the same player), in that order. Curiosity places its bonus Inkling in this book's overflow. Returning a memory lowers its row; an Inkling on it stays. Extra upgrades still suspend Inklings; if impossible, gain 2 points.

## Subplots

Keep your Subplot hidden in your hand. Objectives check the current board and tracks; no counters or recorded progress are used. All stated requirements must be true together. Only your own memories count. Conflict objectives require participation in the current conflict, not earlier battles. After each action or automatic effect, reveal a fulfilled Subplot, choose a character OR its alternative reward, discard it, and draw a new hidden Subplot. At most one Subplot per player may complete in each player turn, including automatic completion effects. Setup never completes Subplots. A replacement waits until a later turn before it can complete. Existing arrangements count; removing pieces can undo an unfinished objective. Twists and Subplots share your hand limit; at the end of your turn you may discard either. An unfinished Subplot discarded for hand limit gives no reward or replacement. Reshuffle discards as needed; played conflict Twists remain aside until scoring.

## Immediate conflict

Finish placement rewards, then immediately fight when the last numbered space gains an Inkling, interrupting further placements. Agamemnon triggers with 1 space still empty; that empty space grants no check. All players with Inklings or figures here participate; lock them in. Reveal the Horse; resolve book/character start effects. Check numbered spaces ONCE, left to right. At each, its CURRENT Inkling owner may play 1 Twist or pass. Skip empty or memory-only spaces; overflow grants no checks. Each owned space is a separate opportunity. Agamemnon/Strategists allow 1 extra Twist at ONE owned check per conflict, not cumulatively. Later spaces use their current occupants; earlier spaces never repeat. Finish even if erasures or upgrades empty spaces. Other full books wait until this conflict finishes.

## Scoring and rewards

After checks, total Inklings (1 each), character power, protagonist Valor and effects. Most power wins; ties favor the earliest participant clockwise from the active player. Discard all played Twists. Resolve win/loss and book after-effects, then clear Inklings according to the book: normally erase them; The Aeneid suspends them; Frankenstein erases old overflow and moves numbered-space Inklings into overflow, where they survive. Memories and figures stay. EVERY conflict takes 1 available current-Act token. Only an uncovered +3 space scores 3 times the NEW token's multiplier (x1/x2/x3); then cover it. A covered book gives NO printed book points. Gain every token's face-up reward in your chosen order: strong bonus or weak 1/2/3 points, never multiplied; flip them weak. The winner then draws and publishes a book touching at least 2 others and places 1 supply Inkling in a touching old book's overflow. No books left: skip publication and its bonus.

## Acts and victory

The last available token ends the Act: finish that conflict, rewards and publication, then end the turn. No conflict resolves without an available token. With no supply at turn START, gain each unused token's strong bonus without book points, multipliers or publication; then end the Act. After Act III, highest points wins; ties share victory. Otherwise flip old tokens strong, uncover each +3 space, return character-card Inklings to supply, release the next reserve, prepare the Horse and display the next Act's selected tokens. Book Inklings stay. Next player starts. Any waiting full book resolves before normal movement.

## Book effects

Dracula: mark the first numbered space with a castle. Its Inkling owner may erase any one Inkling here before Twist checks, including their own or overflow. The Odyssey rewards the moving figure’s owner each time it crosses from this book to another, including ability moves; moving between its two pages gives nothing. Around the World lets the placing player choose their protagonist or any owned character to move one page. Placement effects resolve before checking for a full book. Moving existing Inklings grants no placement rewards. Jekyll may reduce points below zero. The submarine suspends all Inklings sent to overflow, including transfers and Nemo activation Inklings.
